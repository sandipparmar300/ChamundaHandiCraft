Module 11 - Contact
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Contact

Purpose

The Contact module enables customers to communicate with the business through multiple channels including contact forms, WhatsApp, email, phone, Google Maps, and social media. It also manages customer inquiries, support requests, custom product requests, wholesale inquiries, and business communications.

Business Goals
Improve customer communication.
Increase customer trust.
Reduce customer support response time.
Generate business leads.
Support custom handicraft orders.
Handle wholesale and export inquiries.
Increase customer engagement.
Improve customer satisfaction.

30. Recommended .NET 8 Architecture

Implement the Contact module as a dedicated Customer Communication Module within your .NET 8 Modular Monolithic Architecture. Use CQRS to separate inquiry creation from inquiry retrieval. Persist inquiries using Entity Framework Core, while using background services or a message queue to asynchronously send email, SMS, and WhatsApp notifications, ensuring fast response times for customers. Store company contact information in the Settings/CMS module so administrators can update phone numbers, email addresses, business hours, Google Maps location, and social media links without code changes. Design the module with service abstractions (IEmailService, IWhatsAppService, ISmsService, IGoogleMapsService) to support future integrations and enterprise scalability.
