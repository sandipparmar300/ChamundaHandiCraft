Module 4 - Product Details
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Product Details

Purpose

The Product Details module provides customers with comprehensive information about a product, including images, videos, specifications, pricing, availability, reviews, related products, and purchase options. It enables customers to make informed purchasing decisions while providing a rich shopping experience.

Business Goals
Increase product conversion rate.
Improve customer confidence.
Reduce product return rate.
Increase Average Order Value (AOV).
Encourage cross-selling and upselling.
Improve customer engagement.
Enhance SEO visibility.
Provide complete product transparency.

24. Business Rules
Only published products should be visible.
Customers cannot purchase unpublished or disabled products.
Variant selection is mandatory before purchase when variants exist.
Price, SKU, and stock update dynamically based on the selected variant.
Customers cannot add quantities greater than available stock.
Only verified purchasers can submit reviews (configurable).
Only approved reviews are displayed publicly.
Related and similar products are determined by category, tags, brand, and product attributes.
Discounts and offers should be applied in real time.
Shipping and return policies should reflect current system settings.

28. Acceptance Criteria
Customers can view complete product information, images, videos, and specifications.
Product variants dynamically update price, SKU, stock, and images.
Customers can zoom images and view media on desktop and mobile.
Customers can add products to Wishlist, Cart, or proceed directly to Checkout.
Related and Similar Products are displayed based on configurable business rules.
Product reviews are searchable, sortable, and moderated.
Shipping and return information is clearly presented.
Product pages are SEO-friendly, responsive, and accessible.
Analytics accurately track customer interactions.
The page performs efficiently under high traffic and large product catalogs.

Recommended .NET 8 Architecture

Build the Product Details page using a Product Aggregate with separate query services for media, variants, pricing, reviews, recommendations, and inventory. Use CQRS for read operations, Dapper for optimized product detail queries, and Entity Framework Core for transactional updates. Cache product metadata, images, and recommendations using Redis or IMemoryCache, while keeping inventory and pricing real-time. Design reusable components (gallery, variants, reviews, recommendations) so they can be shared across the Customer Website, Mobile App, and future marketplace integrations. This architecture provides enterprise-grade scalability and a premium shopping experience.
