The Coupon Module is much more than applying a discount code. In a professional eCommerce system, it is a complete Promotion Engine that controls discount rules, customer eligibility, product/category applicability, usage limits, scheduling, and analytics. It integrates with Orders, Customers, Products, Categories, Payments, and Reports.

Module 09 - Coupon Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Coupon Management

Purpose

The Coupon Management module allows administrators to create, manage, schedule, validate, and monitor promotional coupon codes. It supports multiple discount types, usage restrictions, customer eligibility, product/category applicability, and complete redemption tracking.

Business Goals
Increase sales and conversions.
Promote new and seasonal products.
Reward loyal customers.
Encourage repeat purchases.
Improve marketing campaign effectiveness.
Reduce manual discount handling.
Track coupon performance and ROI.

19. Business Rules
Coupon codes must be unique.
Coupon codes are case-insensitive during validation.
Coupons cannot be redeemed after their expiry date and time.
Discount cannot exceed the configured maximum discount amount.
Coupon usage limits must be enforced atomically to prevent overuse.
Customers cannot exceed the configured redemption limit.
Coupons can be restricted to specific products, categories, brands, or customer groups.
Only one coupon may be applied per order unless coupon stacking is explicitly enabled.
Cancelled or refunded orders should restore coupon usage based on business configuration.
All coupon actions and redemptions are recorded in the audit log.

23. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can create, edit, schedule, activate, and archive coupons.
Coupon validation correctly enforces eligibility, date ranges, and usage limits.
Flat and percentage discounts are calculated accurately.
Redemption history is maintained for every successful use.
Usage limits are enforced without race conditions.
Reports accurately summarize coupon performance and financial impact.
Notifications are sent for configured coupon events.
All coupon changes and redemptions are logged.
Role-based permissions restrict coupon management operations.
The architecture supports future promotion types without redesign.
