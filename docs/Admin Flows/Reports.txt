Module 18 - Reports & Analytics
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Reports & Analytics

Purpose

The Reports & Analytics module provides comprehensive business insights through interactive dashboards, reports, charts, KPIs, and data exports. It enables administrators and business owners to monitor sales, profits, customer behavior, inventory, payments, taxes, returns, and operational performance for informed decision-making.

20. Business Rules
Reports should reflect only finalized business transactions unless filters specify otherwise.
Date range filtering must be available for every report.
Exported reports must preserve filters, sorting, and grouping.
Financial calculations should use consistent rounding rules.
Tax reports must align with invoice data.
Inventory valuation should support configurable costing methods (Average, FIFO, etc., future enhancement).
Sensitive financial reports should be accessible only to authorized roles.
Scheduled reports should be generated automatically at configured intervals.
Large reports should be processed asynchronously to avoid performance issues.
All report generation and export activities should be recorded in the audit log.

24. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can generate reports across all business domains (Sales, Profit, Customers, Inventory, Products, Tax, Payments, Orders, Returns).
All reports support filtering, searching, sorting, grouping, and drill-down.
Reports can be exported to Excel, CSV, PDF, and printed.
KPI dashboards update using current business data.
Scheduled reports are automatically generated and emailed.
Financial and tax calculations are accurate and auditable.
Role-based permissions restrict access to sensitive reports.
Large datasets are processed efficiently using asynchronous generation.
All reporting activities are logged.
The reporting architecture supports integration with external BI platforms.

Recommended Architecture (.NET 8 Modular Monolith)

To keep the system scalable and maintainable:

Create a dedicated Reporting Module separate from business modules.
Use CQRS (Query Side) for complex report generation.
Use Dapper for high-performance reporting queries instead of Entity Framework for large datasets.
Implement background jobs (Hangfire or Quartz.NET) for scheduled reports.
Cache frequently accessed dashboards using Redis.
Design report definitions to be reusable so the same reporting engine can serve the Admin Panel, future mobile app, and external BI tools.

This approach gives you an enterprise-grade reporting system similar to Shopify, Magento, and Microsoft Dynamics while remaining suitable for your .NET 8 Modular Monolithic architecture.
