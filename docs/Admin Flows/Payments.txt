Module 07 - Payment Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Payment Management

Purpose

The Payment Management module is responsible for managing all payment transactions, payment methods, payment gateway integrations, refunds, reconciliation, and financial transaction history for customer orders.

Business Goals
Support multiple payment methods.
Secure payment processing.
Track every payment transaction.
Simplify refund management.
Reduce payment failures.
Maintain complete financial audit logs.
Enable easy payment reconciliation.

17. Business Rules
Every payment must be linked to a valid order.
Each payment transaction must have a unique transaction ID.
Payment status updates must be driven by gateway responses and webhooks.
COD orders are marked as Pending Payment until collection.
Refunds cannot exceed the original paid amount.
Partial refunds must update the remaining refundable balance.
Successful payments automatically trigger invoice generation and order processing.
Failed payments do not reserve inventory unless explicitly configured.
All gateway responses must be stored for audit purposes.
Sensitive payment information (such as full card numbers or CVV) must never be stored.

21. Acceptance Criteria
Customers can successfully complete payments using supported methods.
Payment statuses update correctly based on gateway responses.
Refunds support both full and partial amounts.
Gateway webhooks are processed securely and idempotently.
Payment history, receipts, and audit logs are available.
Failed payments can be retried without creating duplicate orders.
Reconciliation reports accurately identify mismatches.
Role-based permissions control financial operations.
The module is designed to support additional gateways in the future with minimal changes.
Architecture Recommendation

For your .NET 8 Modular Monolithic Architecture, don't tightly couple payment gateways with business logic. Instead, design a Payment Provider abstraction (e.g., IPaymentGateway) so gateways like Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, Cashfree, PhonePe, or future providers can be added without changing the core payment workflow. This approach will make the system much easier to maintain and extend as your business grows.
