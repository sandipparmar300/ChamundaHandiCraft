Module 08 - Shipping Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Shipping Management

Purpose

The Shipping Management module handles shipping charges, shipping zones, courier integrations, shipment creation, tracking, delivery status, and shipping label generation. It ensures that orders are delivered accurately and efficiently while providing real-time shipment visibility.

Business Goals
Automate shipment processing.
Calculate shipping charges accurately.
Support multiple courier partners.
Track shipments in real time.
Improve delivery performance.
Reduce shipping costs.
Provide customers with shipment visibility.
Maintain complete shipment history.

20. Business Rules
Shipping charges are calculated based on configured rules (weight, order value, quantity, or zone).
Shipping zones determine available couriers and estimated delivery times.
Every shipment must be linked to a valid order.
Tracking numbers must be unique.
Shipping labels cannot be generated until the order is packed.
Delivered shipments are read-only.
Returned shipments update inventory based on inspection results.
Courier webhooks update shipment status automatically.
Failed deliveries can be rescheduled based on business policy.
All shipment events are recorded in the audit log.

24. Acceptance Criteria
Shipping charges are calculated correctly using configured business rules.
Shipping zones control courier availability and pricing.
Couriers can be integrated and managed independently.
Tracking numbers and shipping labels are generated successfully.
Delivery status updates are synchronized with courier systems.
Customers receive shipment notifications at each milestone.
Shipment reports and analytics are accurate.
All shipment events are captured in the audit log.
Role-based permissions control access to shipping operations.
The module is extensible to support new courier providers without changing core business logic.
