Module 14 - Review Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Review Management

Purpose

The Review Management module allows customers to rate and review purchased products while enabling administrators to moderate, approve, reject, and manage customer feedback. It supports verified purchase reviews, review moderation, abuse reporting, media attachments, and review analytics.

Business Goals
Build customer trust.
Improve product credibility.
Increase conversion rates.
Gather customer feedback.
Improve product quality.
Detect fake or abusive reviews.
Enhance SEO through user-generated content.

18. Business Rules
Only customers with completed or delivered orders can submit reviews for purchased products.
A customer can submit only one review per product unless review editing is enabled.
Average product ratings update automatically after approval.
Reviews remain invisible until approved (configurable).
Reviews marked as abusive are hidden until moderation is completed.
Deleted reviews are soft deleted for audit purposes.
Review media must pass file type and size validation.
Verified Purchase badges are assigned only when linked to a delivered order.
All review actions are recorded in the audit log.

22. Acceptance Criteria
Customers can submit ratings and reviews for eligible products.
Ratings are calculated accurately and reflected on product pages.
Reviews support text, images, and videos.
Verified Purchase badges are automatically assigned.
Administrators can approve, reject, hide, restore, and reply to reviews.
Abuse reports are tracked and moderated.
Analytics provide meaningful insights into customer satisfaction.
All review lifecycle events are recorded in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure review moderation.
The architecture supports future AI moderation and advanced engagement features.
