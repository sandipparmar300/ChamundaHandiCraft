Module 11 - Banner Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Banner Management

Purpose

The Banner Management module enables administrators to create, schedule, organize, and publish promotional banners across different sections of the website and mobile devices. It supports homepage banners, sliders, promotional popups, mobile banners, and future campaign placements while providing analytics and click tracking.

Business Goals
Promote products and offers.
Improve customer engagement.
Increase sales through marketing campaigns.
Display seasonal promotions.
Support responsive website design.
Schedule promotional campaigns.
Track banner performance.

21. Business Rules
Banner names should be unique.
A banner must have at least one image before publishing.
Only active banners within the configured schedule are displayed.
Priority determines which banner appears when multiple banners target the same location.
CTA URLs must be validated before publishing.
Images must meet recommended dimensions for each device type.
Popups should respect frequency rules (e.g., once per session or once per user).
Banner impressions and clicks should be recorded for analytics.
Expired banners are automatically hidden.
All banner modifications are recorded in the audit log.

26. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can create, edit, schedule, publish, and archive banners.
Homepage banners, sliders, popups, and mobile banners display correctly on their configured locations.
Banner scheduling automatically activates and deactivates campaigns.
Priority rules resolve conflicts when multiple banners target the same location.
Responsive images are served for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
Banner impressions and clicks are accurately tracked.
CTA links navigate users to the correct destination.
Analytics and reports provide actionable campaign insights.
All banner lifecycle events are recorded in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure banner management.
