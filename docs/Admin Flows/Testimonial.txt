Module 15 - Testimonial Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Testimonial Management

Purpose

The Testimonial Management module enables administrators to collect, manage, approve, publish, and showcase customer testimonials in text, image, and video formats. Testimonials help build credibility, improve customer trust, and increase conversion rates.

Business Goals
Build customer trust.
Increase product credibility.
Showcase customer success stories.
Improve conversion rates.
Highlight artisan craftsmanship.
Strengthen brand reputation.
Support multimedia testimonials.

18. Business Rules
Testimonials can be submitted only by verified customers (configurable).
Customer name and testimonial content are mandatory.
Images and videos must meet file size and format validation rules.
Testimonials remain hidden until approved.
Featured testimonials are displayed according to configured priority.
Video testimonials support upload or external video links.
Deleted testimonials are soft deleted for audit purposes.
Every approval, rejection, and publication event is recorded in the audit log.
Testimonials may optionally include product references and ratings.
Homepage testimonial count should be configurable.

22. Acceptance Criteria
Customers can submit text, image, and video testimonials.
Administrators can review, approve, reject, publish, archive, and feature testimonials.
Testimonials support verified purchase linkage where applicable.
Homepage and CMS pages can display configurable testimonial sections.
Images and videos are validated and optimized.
Analytics accurately measure engagement and performance.
Notifications are sent during the testimonial lifecycle.
All actions are recorded in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure testimonial management.
The architecture supports future multimedia and AI-powered enhancements.

Recommendation

For your handicraft eCommerce platform, enhance testimonials by supporting:

Verified Buyer Badge to increase authenticity.
Product-linked Testimonials, allowing testimonials to appear on both the homepage and the corresponding product detail page.
Image and Video Testimonials, showcasing customers using or displaying handcrafted products in their homes.
Featured Artisan Stories, where customers share the cultural or emotional value of handmade products.

These features will significantly improve trust, engagement, and conversion rates while highlighting the craftsmanship behind your products.
