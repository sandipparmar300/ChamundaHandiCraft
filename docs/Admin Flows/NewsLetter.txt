Module 16 - Newsletter Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Newsletter Management

Purpose

The Newsletter Management module enables administrators to collect subscribers, manage mailing lists, create and send email campaigns, schedule newsletters, monitor campaign performance, and automate email marketing to increase customer engagement and sales.

Business Goals
Grow email subscribers.
Increase repeat purchases.
Promote new products.
Announce offers and festivals.
Drive traffic to blogs.
Build long-term customer relationships.
Improve conversion rates.

20. Business Rules
Email addresses must be unique.
Email format must be validated before subscription.
Double Opt-In (email verification) should be configurable.
Every marketing email must include an unsubscribe link.
Unsubscribed users must not receive future campaigns.
Email templates should support responsive layouts.
Campaign scheduling must use the configured time zone.
Campaign statistics should update in real time where possible.
Imported subscribers should be validated for duplicates.
All newsletter activities are recorded in the audit log.

24. Acceptance Criteria
Visitors can subscribe and unsubscribe from newsletters.
Subscriber information is validated and stored securely.
Administrators can create, schedule, send, and monitor email campaigns.
Campaigns support reusable templates and audience segmentation.
Analytics accurately report delivery, open, click, and conversion metrics.
All marketing emails include unsubscribe functionality.
Imported subscriber lists are validated and deduplicated.
Role-based permissions secure subscriber and campaign management.
All newsletter activities are recorded in the audit log.
The architecture supports future marketing automation and AI-driven personalization.
