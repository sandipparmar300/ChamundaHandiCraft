Module 10 - Offer Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Offer Management

Purpose

The Offer Management module enables administrators to create, schedule, and manage promotional campaigns that are automatically applied during customer shopping. It supports multiple offer types such as festival offers, combo offers, Buy X Get Y promotions, flash sales, category discounts, brand discounts, and future promotional campaigns.

Business Goals
Increase sales during promotional periods.
Boost average order value.
Promote slow-moving inventory.
Increase customer engagement.
Automate promotional campaigns.
Improve seasonal marketing.
Support future marketing strategies.

21. Business Rules
Offer names should be unique.
Only active offers within the configured date and time are applied.
Offer priority determines which promotion is applied when multiple offers are eligible.
Flash Sales automatically start and end based on schedule.
Buy X Get Y promotions validate quantities before applying discounts.
Combo offers require all configured products to be present in the cart.
Automatic offers cannot exceed configured discount limits.
Offer stacking is disabled by default but can be enabled through settings.
If both a coupon and an automatic offer are available, the system follows configurable promotion priority rules.
Every offer application is recorded in the audit log.

25. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can create, edit, activate, schedule, and archive all supported offer types.
Offers are automatically evaluated and applied based on configured rules.
Priority resolution prevents conflicting promotions from producing incorrect discounts.
Festival, Combo, Buy X Get Y, and Flash Sale promotions work independently and together according to stacking rules.
Analytics accurately report revenue, discounts, and redemption metrics.
Notifications are sent for configured offer events.
All offer lifecycle events are captured in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure promotion management.
The architecture supports adding new promotion types without modifying existing business logic.
