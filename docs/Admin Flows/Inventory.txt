Module 05 - Inventory Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Inventory Management

Purpose

The Inventory Management module is responsible for tracking, controlling, and managing product stock across warehouses. It records every inventory movement, including purchases, sales, adjustments, damages, returns, and transfers while maintaining accurate stock availability.

Business Goals
Maintain accurate stock levels.
Prevent overselling.
Track complete inventory movement history.
Improve warehouse efficiency.
Reduce stock shortages.
Monitor damaged and returned products.
Enable barcode-based inventory operations.
Generate inventory reports and alerts.

21. Business Rules
Stock cannot become negative unless negative inventory is explicitly enabled.
Every stock movement must create an inventory ledger entry.
Purchase approval increases stock.
Sales orders reserve or reduce stock based on order status.
Customer returns require inspection before restocking.
Damaged products are excluded from available stock.
Warehouse transfers reduce stock from the source and increase stock at the destination.
Low stock alerts are triggered based on configurable thresholds.
Barcode values must be unique.
All inventory changes are recorded in the audit log.

25. Acceptance Criteria
Stock levels accurately reflect all inventory transactions.
Warehouse-specific inventory is maintained.
Purchase approvals update stock correctly.
Stock adjustments require reasons and are audited.
Damage and return workflows preserve inventory accuracy.
Barcode generation and printing function correctly.
Low stock alerts trigger based on configured thresholds.
Inventory reports and valuation are accurate.
Every stock movement is recorded in the inventory ledger.
Role-based permissions control inventory operations.
Recommendations for a Professional E-Commerce Platform

For a scalable, enterprise-ready inventory system, I recommend adding these capabilities from the beginning:

Stock Reservation – Reserve inventory when an order is placed and release it automatically if the order is cancelled or expires.
Stock Movement Ledger – Maintain an immutable history of every stock transaction for auditing and reconciliation.
Warehouse Bin Locations – Store products by aisle, rack, shelf, and bin to improve picking efficiency.
Batch/Lot & Serial Number Tracking – Useful for premium or limited-edition handcrafted products and future product categories.
Cycle Count & Physical Stock Verification – Schedule inventory counts and reconcile differences without disrupting operations.
Reorder Rules – Configure minimum stock, reorder quantity, lead time, and preferred supplier to support automated purchasing in the future.

These additions will make your inventory module suitable not only for your father's handicraft business today but also for a larger multi-warehouse e-commerce operation as it grows.
