20. Business Rules
Every article must belong to at least one category.
Blog titles and SEO URLs (slugs) must be unique.
Published articles require SEO Title and Meta Description.
Featured image is mandatory for published articles.
Rich text content must be sanitized to prevent XSS attacks.
Comments require moderation before public display (configurable).
Deleted articles are soft deleted.
Scheduled articles are published automatically.
Every content change is recorded in the audit log.
Article view counts should ignore repeated refreshes within a configurable time window.

24. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can create, edit, preview, schedule, publish, archive, and restore blog articles.
Blog categories support hierarchy and custom ordering.
Every article includes SEO metadata and a unique URL.
Rich text editor supports media, formatting, and links.
Comments can be moderated, replied to, and filtered.
Analytics accurately track views, engagement, and performance.
Social sharing works for all published articles.
Scheduled publishing and archiving function automatically.
All blog activities are recorded in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure content creation and moderation.
