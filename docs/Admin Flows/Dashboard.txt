Module 01 - Dashboard
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Dashboard

Purpose

The Dashboard serves as the landing page of the Admin Panel, providing a real-time overview of business performance, operational status, inventory health, customer activities, and recent transactions.

Business Goal
Monitor overall business performance.
Track sales and revenue.
Monitor pending operational tasks.
Identify inventory shortages.
Display recent customer activities.
Provide quick navigation to frequently used modules.
Help management make faster business decisions.

18. Business Rules
Revenue is calculated only from completed orders.
Profit = Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold.
Cancelled orders are excluded from revenue.
Returned orders reduce revenue after refund approval.
Low stock threshold is configurable.
Dashboard refreshes automatically every configurable interval (e.g., 5 minutes).
Data visibility respects user roles and permissions.

20. Acceptance Criteria
Dashboard loads within the defined performance target.
KPI cards display accurate data.
Charts reflect selected filters.
Clicking widgets navigates to the correct module.
Users only see data permitted by their role.
Export functions generate correct reports.
Responsive layout works across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
Auto-refresh updates live metrics without losing applied filters.
