Module 17 - Notification Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Notification Management

Purpose

The Notification Management module enables the system to send real-time and scheduled notifications to customers, administrators, and staff through multiple communication channels, including Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push Notifications, and In-App Notifications. It centralizes notification templates, delivery tracking, scheduling, automation, and user preferences.

Business Goals
Improve customer communication.
Increase customer engagement.
Automate order notifications.
Reduce manual communication.
Improve delivery transparency.
Support marketing campaigns.
Maintain complete notification history.

17. Business Rules
Notification templates must support placeholders (variables).
Every notification is generated from a template.
Failed notifications should automatically retry based on retry policy.
Delivery status must be tracked for every channel.
Customer notification preferences must always be respected for promotional messages.
Transactional notifications (OTP, Order, Payment) override promotional opt-out where legally applicable.
Every notification must be stored in the notification history.
Bulk notifications should be processed using a background queue.
Scheduled notifications should be sent automatically at the configured time.
Every notification event must be recorded in the audit log.

21. Acceptance Criteria
Administrators can create and manage notification templates for all supported channels.
The system automatically sends notifications based on configured business events.
Customers receive notifications through Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push, and future In-App channels.
Notification queues handle retries and failed deliveries automatically.
Delivery logs accurately record the lifecycle of every notification.
Customers can manage notification preferences for promotional communications.
Analytics provide delivery, open, click, and engagement metrics.
All notification activities are recorded in the audit log.
Role-based permissions secure notification management.
The architecture supports future event-driven automation and AI-powered communication.

For your .NET 8 Modular Monolithic Architecture, implement notifications using a background job processor (such as Hangfire or Quartz.NET), a provider abstraction layer, and event-driven domain events. This keeps the system modular today while making it easy to migrate to microservices in the future without redesigning the notification system.
