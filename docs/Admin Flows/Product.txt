Module 03 - Product Management
1. Module Overview
Module Name

Product Management

Purpose

The Product Management module enables administrators to create, organize, manage, publish, and maintain all products available in the e-commerce store. It centralizes product information, pricing, inventory, media assets, SEO, variants, and product visibility.

Business Goals
Maintain a centralized product catalog.
Support handcrafted products with detailed attributes.
Improve customer shopping experience with rich product information.
Simplify inventory and pricing management.
Improve organic search visibility through SEO.
Enable scalable product management for future marketplace expansion.

17. Business Rules
Product Name is mandatory.
SKU must be unique.
Product Code must be unique.
Barcode must be unique.
Selling Price cannot be less than Cost Price unless explicitly allowed.
At least one category is required.
At least one product image is required before publishing.
Published products must have stock status defined.
Variant SKUs must also be unique.
Soft Delete only.
Archived products remain available for reporting but are hidden from customers.

21. Acceptance Criteria
Products can be created, edited, viewed, duplicated, archived, and published.
Unique identifiers (SKU, Product Code, Barcode) are validated.
Variants maintain independent pricing and inventory.
Media uploads support images, videos, and 360° views.
SEO metadata is configurable for every product.
Search, filtering, import, and export work correctly.
Audit logs capture all significant product changes.
Role-based permissions control access to product operations.
Product preview accurately reflects the storefront before publishing.

This scope is designed to support your current handicraft business while also providing a strong foundation for future growth into a larger e-commerce or marketplace platform without major redesign.
